```text
The **`YYMMDD_HHMM_NNN` Timestamping Standard** has been formally specified, implemented, documented, and enforced across the repository.

---

### 1. Specification & Rationale: [docs/TIMESTAMPING_STANDARD.md](file:///home/fekerr/src/edge-ai/docs/TIMESTAMPING_STANDARD.md)

**Format Structure:**
$$\text{\texttt{260720\_0806\_001}} \quad \implies \quad \text{\texttt{[YYMMDD]\_[HHMM]\_[NNN]}}$$

#### Best Practice Rationale & Challenges:
- **Filesystem Safety**: Avoids standard ISO-8601 colons (`:`), which are illegal characters on Windows host filesystems and break cross-platform scripts between WSL2/Linux/Windows.
- **Lexicographical Sortability**: Sorting filenames or log streams alphabetically produces strict chronological order (`ls -1` = timeline order).
- **Intra-Minute Redundancy (`NNN`)**: In fast automated loops where multiple build logs or session steps occur within the same minute (`HHMM`), the 3-digit `NNN` zero-padded counter guarantees uniqueness and ordinal ordering without collisions.
- **4-Digit Year Recommendation**: Notes that 4-digit years (`YYYYMMDD`) are recommended for long-term external archives to eliminate century ambiguity.

---

### 2. Sequence Counter (`NNN`) Lifecycle & Human Overrides

1. **Auto-Increment**: `NNN` begins at `001` and increments (`001`, `002`, `003`...) for each asset generated within a session or time block.
2. **Auto-Reset**: Resets to `001` when the date/time block shifts or a new major task begins.
3. **Human Override**: A human maintainer can pass `--seq-start <N>` to any tool (e.g. `python3 tools/sync_agy_logs.py --seq-start 001`) to explicitly specify where `NNN` starts.

---
```
